# Code of Conduct

DrawBase is committed to a respectful, inclusive and technically constructive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and communicate with empathy.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Give and accept actionable feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials, schema data or personal information.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, spam and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, pull requests and other contributions that violate this code. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct issues privately through the maintainer's contact options on the GitHub profile. Do not use the public issue tracker for sensitive reports. Reports will be reviewed fairly, kept as confidential as practical and handled without retaliation against good-faith reporters.

This code applies to all DrawBase project spaces and to public representation of the project.
